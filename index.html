Reflection: Non-Verbal Communication Group Assignment

Before you start

This week, you worked in a group of three on the assignment “Say It Without Words.” You learned about eye contact, facial expressions, body language and gestures, researched gestures from different cultures, and chose the right non-verbal response in different situations.

You did the assignment as a group, but this reflection is your own. Answer the questions below in complete sentences. Be honest about your experience — there are no right or wrong answers here. Aim for three to four sentences per question.

1 What was the most interesting or surprising thing you learned about non-verbal communication in this lesson? Why was it interesting to you?

Think about: eye contact (the 3.3-second study), facial expressions, fight/flight/freeze, mirroring, or the thumbs-up and OK gestures.

0 words

2 Your group researched two gestures from different cultures. Which gesture did you find the most interesting, and what did it teach you about communicating with people from other countries?

Think about: what the gesture means, where it is used, and why it matters at work.

Think about: the quiz, the reading, the gesture research, or the “Show Me the Right Move” scenarios.
